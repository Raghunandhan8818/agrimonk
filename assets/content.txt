FOUNDER SECTION

Meet The Founder

Monish Gowda

Agripreneur | Agriculture Consultant | Business Strategist

Monish Gowda founded Agri Monk India with a vision to modernize the image of agriculture and help more people confidently enter farming and agribusiness.

With a background in agribusiness management from ICAR-NAARM and industry experience with DCM Shriram Ltd and ITC Limited, he combines agriculture, business strategy, and modern thinking to help people discover opportunities in farming.

His mission is simple:

“Make agriculture exciting, practical, and profitable for the next generation.”
